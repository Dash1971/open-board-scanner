Fix a square: point at it on either board and press p n b r q k for a black piece, the same key with Shift for a white one, Space or x for empty, c to swap its colour. Or click the square, then a piece below the board.

Red squares are ones the model was unsure of; blue ones you changed. The orange edge on the photo is the top of the board.
